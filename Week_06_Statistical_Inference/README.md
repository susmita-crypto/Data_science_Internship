\# Week 6 - Statistical Inference and Hypothesis Testing



\## Overview



This week focused on statistical inference, confidence intervals, p-values, hypothesis testing, and basic A/B testing.



The cleaned Titanic dataset from Week 5 was used for practical statistical analysis.



\## Topics Covered



\- Statistical inference

\- Confidence intervals

\- Margin of error

\- P-values

\- Hypothesis testing

\- A/B testing

\- Independent samples t-test

\- Chi-square test

\- Plain-language interpretation of statistical results



\## Dataset



The analysis uses the cleaned Titanic dataset created during Week 5.



Dataset path:



`../Week\_05\_EDA\_Visualization/data/titanic\_clean.csv`



\## Statistical Analysis



\### Confidence Interval



A 95% confidence interval was calculated for the mean age of Titanic passengers.



\### Independent Samples t-Test



An independent samples t-test was used to compare the mean age of male and female passengers.



\### Chi-Square Test



A chi-square test was used to examine the association between passenger sex and survival.



\### A/B Testing



A simple example was used to understand how statistical testing can compare two groups.



\## Key Learning Outcomes



\- Understand the purpose of statistical inference.

\- Understand what a p-value represents.

\- Apply hypothesis testing using a significance level of 0.05.

\- Perform an independent samples t-test.

\- Perform a chi-square test.

\- Understand the basic idea of A/B testing.

\- Interpret statistical results in plain language.
